We are always looking for new members — whether you're new to singing or experienced.

Become Part of the Choir

Felling Male Voice Choir is a welcoming, community-focused choir. We rehearse every Friday night and welcome new members regardless of experience. Come along and find your voice section — we'll help you settle in.

Rehearsals

When: Fridays, 19:00–21:30

Where: Felling Methodist Church, Coldwell St, Felling, Gateshead, NE10 9HH

View on Google Maps

You can turn up to a rehearsal to see what it's like, or get in touch first if you prefer. We're open and welcoming.

Get in Touch

If you'd like to join us, email join@fmvc.org or contact us via the contact page or social media. We look forward to hearing from you.

Contact us about joining

Become a Patron

Interested in becoming a patron of the choir? Patronage is £10 per year. As a thank you you receive two free tickets for the patrons' concert. For more information contact patron@fmvc.org.
