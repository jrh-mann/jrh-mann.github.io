WELCOME to the Felling Male Voice Choir website

Why not come and join us and enjoy some singing, friendship and good craic?

The following pages will give you a flavour of what we are all about. Please take a little time to read them and listen to some of our music, and then decide whether you might like to become part of this well known and friendly choir.

Everyone is welcome at our rehearsals, which take place on Friday evenings from 7:00 pm at Felling Methodist Church Hall. Whether you are an experienced singer or simply enjoy a good sing, you will be made very welcome.

We look forward to seeing you soon. In the meantime, enjoy the rest of the website.
